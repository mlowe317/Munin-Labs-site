How we work

Security is the default, not the upsell.

Most vendors treat security as a tier you pay extra for. We treat it as the starting point. Every engagement, whatever its size, gets the same controls.

  1. Understand the risk

    We start with your data, your regulators and your worst-case scenario. That shapes the architecture before anything is built.

  2. Build inside your perimeter

    On-premises, in your VPC, or in an isolated tenant. You own the keys, the logs and the data.

  3. Ship fast, prove it works

    Short, measurable milestones. You see working software early and often, with evidence at every step.

  4. Hand over, or stay on

    Full documentation and runbooks so your team can run it. Or we run it for you under a clear service agreement.
